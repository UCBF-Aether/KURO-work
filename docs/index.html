FlyBrain² — The Fly-Brain Map

The fly-brain map · Code

Free Drosophila connectome wiring map for exploration and research.

What is it?
The full wiring map of a real fruit fly brain — all 138,000 neurons and every connection between them, built from the actual Drosophila connectome.
What does it do?
Gives you the brain’s wiring as data you can build on — study the circuits, train a model on them, or wire them into your own AI. It’s the map, not an app.
Who is it for?
Researchers, students, and builders who want a real brain to work from instead of a toy model.
What do I need?
A computer that can open a zip file. Nothing else.
How do I use it?
Download the map and read the setup guide.

Good to knowA map, not an app — you bring the use.

KUROdoctor

A doctor for devices · Windows · Linux

Free AI-assisted PC diagnostic and repair tool for Windows and Linux.

What is it?
A doctor for a PC that won’t boot or is acting sick. It goes in blind — no working OS needed — finds what’s broken on its own, and shows you every fix before it touches anything. You approve each move.
What does it do?
Finds the real problem — bad drive, broken boot, corrupted files — and repairs it step by step, with your go-ahead on every fix. No shop, no bill, no data leaving your machine.
Who is it for?
Anyone with a sick PC who would rather fix it than pay a shop.
What do I need?
A Windows or Linux PC. The packs are blank — you bring your own relay.
How do I use it?
Download your pack. The setup guide (SETUP.md) is inside every pack.

Good to knowNothing changes without you seeing it first. Windows and Linux only — the Mac build was pulled because it was never tested on a real Mac.

KUROdoctor Authenticator

Session approvals · Android

Free Android app that lets you approve or deny KUROdoctor sessions from your phone.

What is it?
The approval key for your doctor. When Muse asks to work on your device, your phone buzzes and you tap Approve or Deny — no codes, no texts, no one else in the middle.
What does it do?
Lets you approve or deny a repair session from your phone, and keeps watch for requests even with the app closed.
Who is it for?
KUROdoctor users who run their own relay.
What do I need?
An Android phone, plus your own relay URL and pairing code.
How do I use it?
Install the app, enter your relay URL and pairing code once — it pairs in a minute.

Good to knowA session only starts when you tap Approve. The Lock button ends a session early.

Signal Scanner

Live radio scanner · Android

Android app for viewing live wireless and radio measurements from device sensors.

What is it?
A live radio scanner for Android. It reads your phone’s own radios — Wi-Fi, cellular, GNSS, Bluetooth, NFC, UWB — and shows you what’s actually in the air around you, right now. Nothing from databases, no guesses.
What does it do?
Shows live signal strength, towers, and radios around you, measured by your phone in real time — so you can see what’s really there instead of trusting a coverage map.
Who is it for?
Android users curious about the radios around them.
What do I need?
An Android phone.
How do I use it?
Get the app and read the setup guide.

Good to knowReadings are live measurements from your phone’s own sensors only. They depend on your device and are not guaranteed to be comprehensive.
