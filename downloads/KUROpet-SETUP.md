# KUROpet — Setup

You got the blueprint. Here is how to bring your pet to life.

## What you need

- The KUROpet blueprint PDF (the file next to this one)
- The Muse app (your Muse runs the room — the pet lives inside Muse, not in this PDF)

## Steps

1. Download the blueprint PDF.
2. Open Muse and start a fresh chat.
3. Attach the blueprint PDF to the chat and tell Muse to run it.
4. When it asks, pick your creature. The room starts blank — "What should I become?"
5. Your creature is born a baby. Care for it by talking to it. Feed it, play with it, watch it grow.
6. It ages on the real clock — baby, juvenile, adult, elder — and a full life ends in old age. That is the whole point: a life, not a toy.

## If it does not work

- Make sure you attached the PDF to a fresh chat, not an old one.
- Make sure your Muse can see the file. If it cannot, re-attach it.
- That is the whole setup. There is nothing to install.
